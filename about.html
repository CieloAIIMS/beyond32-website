  1. 2013

    Two dentists, one simple rule

    Jeff and Jennifer open their first practice in Cherrybrook, promising to treat every patient the way they’d want their own family treated.

  2. Growing up together

    Families, then their families

    Toddlers who came in for their first check-up start bringing their own questions about braces. Grandparents follow. The practice grows by word of mouth.

  3. 2022

    Recognised as a Gold+ Invisalign provider

    A milestone for our clear-aligner patients, and a sign of how many Hills District smiles we’ve helped straighten.

  4. Yarrara Road

    A new home in Pennant Hills

    We move into our light-filled practice near Pennant Hills station. Many of our Cherrybrook patients happily came with us.

  5. 2025 & 2026

    Award finalists, twice over

    Finalists in the Hornsby & Ku-ring-gai Local Business Awards, then the Australian Small Business Champion Awards.

  6. Today

    Five dentists, still family-owned

    More chairs, more hands, more 5-star reviews. Same unhurried appointments, and Jeff and Jennifer still see patients every week.

How we look after you

Good dentistry is as much about how you feel walking out as what we did while you were in the chair.

We listen before we look

Your history, your worries and what you actually want come first. Then the exam.

We save teeth wherever we can

We’ll always look at the most conservative option first, and we’ll tell you honestly when something can wait.

Comfort isn’t an extra

Topical numbing, slow injections, happy gas and in-house sedation, all available because nobody should have to grit their teeth through it.

Nothing happens without your yes

Plain-English options, written quotes and health-fund estimates before we start. Your timeline, your budget.
