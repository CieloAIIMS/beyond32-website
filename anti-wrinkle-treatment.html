Here’s how it works

  1. The consultation

    During a consultation, we’ll ask you what you want to get from your anti-wrinkle treatment. Is it to banish those tell-tale frown lines between your eyes or eradicate crow’s feet? Alternatively, is it to eradicate dynamic lines and wrinkles that have recently appeared when you smile? Either way, tell us what you’d like to achieve and we’ll tell you exactly how we’re going to go about making it happen.

    During the session, our dentist will also answer any questions or concerns you may have and will explain how cosmetic injectables work. When you’re happy and good to go, we’ll get you to sign a consent form and then we’re ready to proceed.

  2. Application of a topical gel

    Topical numbing gel or cream isn’t always needed, as the cosmetic injections are very fine and quick. However, if you haven’t undergone any form of anti-wrinkle treatment before and are understandably a little anxious or nervous, we’re happy to apply a localised numbing cream for your complete comfort.

    Our husband and wife team, Dr Jeff Lin and Dr Jennifer Chung, can assure our patients that they are in a safe pair of hands.

  3. Cosmetic injections

    Once everyone is ready to proceed, our skilled clinicians will carefully start the anti-wrinkle treatment.

    During this process, a safe and clinically proven natural protein known as a neurotoxin is injected into the area. This is done using a series of fine needle injections. Designed to block the signals from the nerves to the muscles, it means that, temporarily at least, the affected muscles can no longer fully contract. As a result, any tell-tale frown or laughter lines will be softened.

    Neurotoxins are available in the market under various brand names, which work equally well. As a general rule of thumb, the injection process should take no longer than 15 minutes.

  4. Ice packs and advice

    That’s it, quick and easy, and over in a flash! Now, all that’s left to do is to apply a cold compress or ice pack to the injection site if needed, and to give advice on anti-wrinkle care. Once that’s done, patients can be on their way.

    The whole process takes just 45 minutes, so for busy local residents, the good news is that treatment can be fitted in within a lunch hour, making it the ideal speedy rejuvenation treatment. In addition, the vast majority of people are able to return to work without the need to take time off. So if you fancy perking up your jaded appearance in super-quick time, give us a call.

In addition, they understand what makes great facial symmetry and can deliver anti-wrinkle treatment that enhances the look of a smile, rather than overpowering it.

When will I see results?

Effects begin within 48 hours of injection, but typically they become noticeable within 5–10 days.

You say anti-wrinkle treatment is temporary. How long does it last?

Treatments usually last somewhere between 3–5 months, so patients can expect to go back for top-up treatments after this time.
