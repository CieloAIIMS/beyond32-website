H1: Crown and Bridges
P: Just like everything else in the world, teeth after years of services will start to break down. The most common reasons for break down of tooth are:
H2: 1. Tooth decay that causes destruction of tooth structure,
P: 2. Accidents that causes tooth crack or fracture.
P: Once a significant amount of natural tooth structure is missing, the remaining tooth structure is weakened. A dental crown or porcelain onlay will be the ideal treatment option for the long survival for the tooth. Dental crowns, made of superior strong ceramic material, wrap around the weaken tooth like a helmet.
P: There are many different type of crown, ranging from gold to many different types of white crowns. The material chosen is based on the biting forces, location of the tooth, and of course aesthetic consideration! Your dentist at Beyond 32 Dental can provide a comprehensive assessment and determine the most suitable crown for you.
P: A bridge is an option to replace missing teeth. It consists of two crowns going on top of your natural teeth with a false crown/pontic in the middle. It literally bridges the gap and maintain the integrity of the two neighbouring teeth especially when they are heavily filled.
