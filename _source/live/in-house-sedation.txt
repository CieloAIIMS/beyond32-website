H2: In-house sedation – What we offer
P: The two types of sedation available at Beyond 32 Dental are:
LI: IV Sedation – which is administered via a small cannula placed on the back of the hand and,
LI: Inhalation sedation, which is commonly known as happy gas.
P: Each method delivers good results and allows patients to have the treatment they need while placing them in a relaxed and happy state.
H3: The benefits of offering sedation dentistry in Pennant Hills are as follows
H3: Reduction in the number of visits
P: Some nervous or anxious dental patients find it hard to even sit in the dental chair, let alone have treatment, so even having one treatment can be a real push. With in-house sedation dentistry, however, patients can have several or more treatments while remaining safe under sedation.
H3: You’ll be feeling pretty good
P: They don’t call it happy gas for nothing -it’s true! Nitrous oxide – the gas used for RA treatments and other sedatives used in IV treatments, is designed to put you in a happy, relaxed state. Most people don’t know much about the treatment, but many people do experience feelings of euphoria and a gentle floating sensation.
H3: Zero after effects
P: Some people feel sick after receiving general anaesthetic and it can stay in your system for hours – even days. Happy gas, on the other hand, gives you zero after effects, so patients will be back to normal pretty quickly after the event.
P: IV sedation doesn’t work quite as instantaneously but patients should be back to normal within the hour. For this reason, it may be okay to drive home after undergoing happy gas, but you will need someone to drive you if IV sedation is carried out. Either way, consult with Dr Lin or Dr Chung first.
H3: It’s not a general anaesthetic
P: Sedation is not a general anaesthetic and as a result, any risks are virtually non-existent. Instead, patients who are coming out of sedation tend to feel very relaxed and generally aware of their surroundings.
P: In-house sedation here in Pennant Hills can be safely used on adults and children alike across a wide range of treatments.
