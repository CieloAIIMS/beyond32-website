H3: Anti-wrinkle Treatment in Cherrybrook
H1: Rejuvenate Your Skin As Well As Your Smile
P: The truth is that many dentists – including our highly skilled team here at Beyond 32 Dental – are taking more of a holistic approach to dental care – and, when you look at it in this context, it makes perfect sense.
P: Let’s face it, you wouldn’t place a great work of art like a Picasso or a Rembrandt in a cheap ply-wood frame. So likewise, it doesn’t make sense to take the time and money needed to perfect a great-looking smile, only for it to be framed by tired and jaded skin.
P: Our experienced team can take your smile and give it the frame it deserves by offering a range of non-surgical treatments such as anti-wrinkle facial rejuvenation. Anti-ageing/Anti-wrinkle Treatment in Cherrybrook – What patients can expect When patients visit our modern Cherrybrook dental clinic for anti-wrinkle treatment, it can either be as part of a series of treatments designed to deliver a full smile makeover or as a stand-alone treatment in its own right.
P: Either way, patients can expect the same level of caring and compassionate service, skilfully delivered by highly trained professionals.
P: Here’s how it works… Step 1 – The Consultation During a consultation, we’ll ask you what you want to get from your anti-wrinkle treatment. Is it to banish those tell-tale frown lines between your eyes or eradicate crow’s feet? Alternatively, is it to eradicate dynamic lines and wrinkles that have recently appeared when you smile? Either way, tell us what you’d like to achieve and we’ll tell you exactly how we’re going to go about making it happen.
P: During the session, our dentist will also answer any questions or concerns you may have and will explain how cosmetic injectables work. When you’re happy and good to go, we’ll get you to sign a consent form and then we’re ready to proceed.
H4: Step 2 – Application of a Topical gel
P: Topical numbing gel or cream isn’t always needed as the cosmetic injections are very fine and quick. However, if you haven’t undergone any form of anti-wrinkle treatment before and are understandably a little anxious or nervous, we’re happy to apply a localised numbing cream for your complete comfort. Our husband and wife team, Dr Jeff Lin and Dr Jennifer Chung, can assure our patients that they are in a safe pair of hands.
H4: Step 3 – cosmetic injections
P: Once everyone is ready to proceed, our skilled clinicians will carefully start the anti-wrinkle treatment.
P: During this process, a safe and clinically proven natural protein known as a neurotoxin is injected into the area. This is done using a series of fine needle injections. Designed to block the signals from the nerves to the muscles, it means that – temporarily at least – the affected muscles can no longer fully contract. As a result, any tell-tale frown or laughter lines will be softened.
P: Neurotoxins are available in the market under various brand names, which work equally well. As a general rule of thumb, the injection process should take no longer than 15 minutes.
H4: Step 4 - Ice packs and advice
P: That’s it, quick and easy, and over in a flash! Now, all that’s left to do is to apply a cold compress or ice pack to the injection site if needed – and to give advice on anti-wrinkle care. Patients should know that once that’s done, they can be on their way.
P: The whole process takes just 45 minutes, so for busy local residents, the good news is that treatment can be fitted in within a lunch hour, making it the ideal speedy rejuvenation treatment. In addition, the vast majority of people are able to return to work without the need to take time off. So if you fancy perking up your jaded appearance in super-quick time, give us a call.
