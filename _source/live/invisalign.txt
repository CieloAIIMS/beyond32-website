H1: Invisalign in Pennant Hills
P: Straight, crowded or gappy teeth affect more than appearance. Misalignment can make teeth harder to clean, place an uneven load on the jaw joint, and wear down enamel over time. Invisalign is a clear aligner system that moves teeth gradually using a series of custom-made, removable trays instead of fixed brackets and wires.
P: At Beyond 32 Dental, we’ve been providing Invisalign treatment since the practice opened in 2013. Treatment starts with a digital intraoral scan rather than a physical mould, so you can see a projection of your expected tooth movement before you commit to anything. Aligners are worn 20 to 22 hours a day and swapped for the next set roughly every one to two weeks.
P: We’re a five-minute drive from West Pennant Hills and Cherrybrook, with three hours of free parking on site.
