H2: WHAT ARE DENTAL VENEERS IN PENNANT HILLS?
P: Veneers are thin, custom-made shells designed to cover the front surface of teeth, enhancing their appearance and creating a more polished smile. These durable restorations effectively address a range of dental imperfections, including chips, gaps, and discolouration, providing a transformative solution.
P: Porcelain veneers are crafted from high-quality ceramic that mimics the natural translucency of tooth enamel. Renowned for their durability and stain resistance, porcelain veneers are an excellent choice for long-lasting enhancement. They blend seamlessly with surrounding teeth to create a natural look.
P: In contrast, composite veneers are made from a tooth-coloured resin that can be sculpted directly onto the teeth. They may last less than porcelain veneers but offer a more affordable option. They can often be completed in a single visit, making them a versatile choice for quick smile adjustments. Want to discuss veneers with our dental professionals?
