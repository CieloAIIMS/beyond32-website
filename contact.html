Good to know before you come

No referral needed

Just call or book online. New patients are always welcome, including the whole family in back-to-back appointments.

Claim on the spot

We accept all major health funds, including Medibank, Bupa, HCF, NIB and CBHS, so you only pay the gap on the day.

Bring your health fund card

Plus a list of any medications. Arrive about ten minutes early for your first visit so there’s no rush.
