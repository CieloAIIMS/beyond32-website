Your first visit

We know new patient nerves are real, so here’s exactly what happens.

Book Your First Visit

  1. 01. A proper conversation first

    Before anything else, we sit down and talk through your history, your concerns, and what “a healthy smile” actually means to you.

  2. 02. A thorough exam, not a rushed glance

    3D X-rays where needed, a full check of your teeth, gums, bite and jaw, and an honest run-through of what we find.

  3. 03. A plan you actually understand

    No jargon, no pressure. We’ll explain your options in plain English and let you decide what’s next, on your timeline and your budget.

  4. 04. Comfort, from the first minute

    If dental visits make you anxious, tell us. We’ll adjust the pace, the chair, the sedation options, whatever helps you get through it.

We’re here to answer your questions, ease your concerns and help you feel confident about your care.

How much do dental implants cost?

It depends on how many teeth are being replaced and what’s needed to prepare the area. We give you a written quote at your consultation. See our current dental implant offer and dental implants.

How much are veneers?

It depends on the material and how many teeth are involved. Composite veneers cost less than porcelain. You’ll get an itemised, written quote at your cosmetic consultation. More on veneers.

How much does Invisalign cost?

It depends on how much your teeth need to move. Your Invisalign consultation includes a digital scan, so you can see the projected result and the cost before you commit, and payment plans are available. See Special Offers.

Is root canal treatment painful?

The tooth is numbed before we begin, so you should feel pressure rather than pain. Root canal treatment is usually done to relieve the pain an inflamed or infected nerve is causing. More on root canal therapy.

How long does teeth whitening last?

Anywhere from several months to a couple of years. Coffee, tea, red wine and smoking bring the stain back faster, and occasional top-ups with take-home trays keep it where you want it. More on teeth whitening.

How much are braces?

It depends on the type of braces and how long treatment takes. We’ll explain your options, give you a written quote, and payment plans are available. See teeth straightening options.

Do you offer emergency dental appointments?

Yes. Call us on (02) 9158 6334 and we’ll get you seen with same-day appointments wherever possible. See what to do right now.

What if I’m nervous about the dentist?

Tell us. We’ll adjust the pace, the chair and the sedation options, with happy gas and in-house sedation available for any procedure. More on in-house sedation.
