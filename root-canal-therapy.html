There could be something wrong with the nerve of the tooth.

What is the dental pulp?

The dental pulp is the nerve in the centre of a tooth. Other than providing hot and cold sensation, the dental pulp also nourishes the tooth and makes it less prone to fracture.

  • Provides hot and cold sensation
  • Nourishes the tooth
  • Makes it less prone to fracture

When the nerve is in trouble

In the event of trauma, a deep restoration or large decay, the dental pulp may die or become irritated, which forms a breeding ground for bacteria.

  • Trauma
  • A deep restoration
  • Large decay

This can cause extreme pain and sometimes an abscess or swelling, even before you realise there is a problem.

How root canal therapy helps

A root canal therapy (or nerve treatment) aims to eliminate the bacteria inside the canals of the problematic tooth that would otherwise need removal.
